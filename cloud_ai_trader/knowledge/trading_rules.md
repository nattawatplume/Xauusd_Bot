# Trading Rules (AI-Managed)

> Last updated: Optimized for natural, balanced trade execution on XAUUSD M5
> Maintained by the AI to balance risk preservation with active opportunity capture.

## Entry Rules
1. Identify market regime (Strong Trend, Moderate Trend, Breakout, or Range).
2. In Uptrends (EMA50 > EMA200 or ADX > 20 with bullish slope):
   - Look for Buy entries on pullbacks toward EMA21 or when RSI dips into 45-52 and bounces.
   - Or enter on confirmed Breakouts above recent 12-bar swing highs.
3. In Downtrends (EMA50 < EMA200 or ADX > 20 with bearish slope):
   - Look for Sell entries on pullbacks toward EMA21 or when RSI bounces into 48-55 and rejects.
   - Or enter on confirmed Breakouts below recent 12-bar swing lows.
4. Do not chase price when RSI is in extreme overbought (>75) for Buy or extreme oversold (<25) for Sell.
5. Only pause for High-impact news (NFP, FOMC, CPI) right at the release window.
6. Max spread allowed: 40 points (0.40 USD).

## Exit Rules
1. Use dynamic ATR-based stop loss (1.2x - 1.5x ATR).
2. Target 1.5R to 2.0R reward-to-risk.
3. Maximum holding duration: 16 bars (approx. 1 hour 20 min on M5).
4. Break-even: Lock entry price when profit reaches 1.0R.

## Risk Management (Guardrails)
1. Per-trade risk cap: 5% ($5 on $100 account).
2. Daily loss cap: 8% ($8 on $100 account).
3. Maximum 5 trades per day.
